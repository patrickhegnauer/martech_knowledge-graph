Ontology

This project models business meaning (journeys, KPIs, requirements) alongside technical metadata (XDM components, data layer variables) as one connected graph. The schema is defined in martech-ontology.ttl — RDFS-level only, intentionally: no OWL reasoning or constraints, just classes and properties.

The core chain

Requirement → KPI → Journey → Stage → Feature → Component → DataLayerVariable — from business question down to raw implementation.

A Requirement is the business problem or hypothesis that justifies tracking something. It's addressed_by a KPI, the measurable objective. A Journey has_stages in order, and each Stage can use_feature a reusable Feature and rolls_up_to a KPI. A Component is a metric or dimension from the semantic layer (CJA Semantic Layer MCP) — its identity stays authoritative in AEP, but its business meaning (definition, caveats, context, owner) is curated here, since a platform's own technical metadata graph structurally has no field for that. A DataLayerVariable is the raw implementation artifact (e.g. a data layer field) that maps_to the component it populates.

Why Measurement and StageTransition exist

A plain RDF predicate connects two resources with no attribute of its own. Some relationships in this model need one — a StageTransition carries a conversion_rate, and a Measurement (a "measured_by" link between a Stage or Feature and a Component) can carry a filter_value (e.g. a specific page-name value that identifies the stage). Both are modeled as their own nodes instead of direct triples, following the W3C n-ary relations pattern. Neither is a "real" business entity — they exist purely to hold that extra attribute.

Classes

ClassComment
RequirementThe business problem, question, or hypothesis that justifies tracking something.
KPIA measurable objective that addresses a requirement.
JourneyA named, end-to-end customer path.
StageAn ordered step within a journey.
FeatureA reusable functional capability of the product. Not sequential; can be used by multiple stages or journeys.
ComponentA metric or dimension from the semantic layer (CJA Semantic Layer MCP). Identity is authoritative in AEP; business meaning (definition, caveats, context) is curated here.
DataLayerVariableThe raw implementation artifact (e.g. a data layer field) that populates a component.

MeasurementA "measured_by" relationship between a Stage or Feature and a Component, optionally qualified by a filter value (e.g. a specific page-name value that identifies the stage).
